The post-pandemic shift is real and it is structural. People are not going back to sedentary lives. The conversation is moving from managing decline to building capacity. From nursing homes to gyms and parks, from convenience to movement, from cushioned dependency to the radical idea that the body was already equipped for this. Vivobarefoot is not selling footwear in this moment. It is selling the correction. The East Coast launch is the moment that proof reaches its biggest market yet.

Commercial Strategy
East Coast Wholesale Plan
Priority Accounts
  • AnchorREI Already carries Vivobarefoot online. Priority is in-store placement at NYC, Boston, and DC flagships. Staff education program drives floor conversion.
  • Tier 1Fleet Feet 30-plus East Coast doors. Running specialty with high-trust staff recommendations. The Primus line is built for this channel.
  • Tier 1Running Independents NYC Running Co., JackRabbit, Pacers DC, Boston Running Center. Smaller volume, higher advocacy. These stores make lifers.
  • NewWellness Boutiques CorePower, YogaWorks. Shop-in-shop for the yoga community already training barefoot. Easiest conversion of any wholesale channel.
  • NewPT Clinics Practitioner recommendation program. PT referrals carry more conversion authority than any ad. Patients trust their physio more than they trust marketing.
  • NewOutdoor Specialty EMS, MEC, outdoor independents from Maine to Miami. Trail and outdoor line focus.
The Operating Principle

Vivobarefoot already has the data. The product converts. The job is not to build belief in the brand. It is to put that proof in front of the right accounts and execute with the discipline that makes partnerships last. Monthly trading reviews. Sell-through tracking. Margin discipline from day one. Quarterly wholesale calendar aligned with SoHo activations so retail and wholesale amplify each other rather than compete for the same buyer.

Shop-in-Shop Standard
Minimum qualified footfall. Trained brand advocate on staff. Values alignment with the movement mission. Not volume for its own sake. The wrong account at the wrong price point sets the brand back.
